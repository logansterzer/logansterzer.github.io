# Privacy Policy for AI Teacher

**Last Updated: August 19, 2026**

AI Teacher ("we," "our," or "the App") is an educational application designed to help students learn through AI-powered tutoring, quizzes, homework assistance, personalized study plans, and other educational tools.

This Privacy Policy explains what information AI Teacher may collect, how we use it, and the choices available to users.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use AI Teacher, we may collect:

### Account Information

If the App requires an account, we may collect information such as:

* Email address
* Name or username
* Account identifier

### Educational Information

When you use AI Teacher, you may provide:

* Questions and messages sent to the AI tutor
* Homework questions or assignments
* Quiz answers and results
* Study preferences
* Learning progress
* Information you voluntarily provide to personalize your learning experience

### Photos and Uploaded Content

If you use features such as Homework Scanner, you may choose to upload photographs or other images of homework or educational materials.

We use uploaded content to provide the requested educational assistance.

### Subscription and Purchase Information

If you purchase AI Teacher Pro, payment processing is handled by Apple App Store, Google Play, or other authorized payment providers.

We may receive information necessary to determine whether your subscription is active, such as your subscription status and transaction-related identifiers.

We do not receive or store your full credit card number through the App.

### Usage and Technical Information

We may collect information necessary to operate, secure, and improve the App, which may include:

* Device information
* App version
* Crash and diagnostic information
* General usage information
* Anonymous or pseudonymous identifiers

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

* Provide AI tutoring and educational features
* Answer questions and provide homework assistance
* Generate quizzes and practice exams
* Create personalized study recommendations
* Track learning progress
* Process and manage subscriptions
* Maintain and improve the App
* Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized use
* Diagnose technical problems
* Provide customer support
* Comply with applicable legal requirements

We do not sell users' personal information.

## 3. AI Services

AI Teacher uses third-party artificial intelligence services to provide some educational features.

Information you submit to AI Teacher, such as questions, prompts, homework content, or other educational material, may be transmitted to these services when necessary to provide the requested functionality.

We encourage users not to submit highly sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for the educational task.

## 4. Third-Party Services

AI Teacher may use third-party services to operate the App, including services for:

* Artificial intelligence processing
* Subscription management and payment processing
* App analytics and diagnostics
* Authentication
* Cloud storage or hosting

These third-party providers may process information on our behalf and are expected to protect information consistent with applicable privacy requirements.

Apple and Google may independently collect and process information according to their own privacy policies when you use their platforms or purchase subscriptions.

## 5. Subscriptions

AI Teacher may offer an optional paid subscription called AI Teacher Pro.

Subscriptions are purchased through the applicable app store and automatically renew unless canceled according to the store's subscription rules.

RevenueCat may be used to manage subscription status and determine whether a user has access to Pro features.

RevenueCat may process information necessary to manage purchases and subscription entitlements.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain the App, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

## 7. Deleting Your Information

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information by contacting us using the contact information provided below.

Some information may need to be retained when required by law or for legitimate business purposes, such as preventing fraud or resolving disputes.

## 8. Children's Privacy

AI Teacher is an educational application and may be used by students.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to us in a manner that violates applicable law, they may contact us and request that the information be reviewed and deleted.

Parents and guardians may contact us regarding privacy concerns involving a minor.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

* Request correction of inaccurate information
